PROJECT NOTEBOOK / 02

ClassEcho

An AI classroom-assistant idea: help make a lesson easier to revisit once the class is over.

AI Thon idea / not deployed
EducationAI-assisted conceptClass 9 / 2025
THE QUESTION

What if a student could return to the important parts of a lesson in a form that made sense to them?

01 / The problem I noticed

Students can forget parts of a lesson or find it hard to catch up later. At a school AI Thon in Class 9, I wanted to explore whether an AI classroom assistant could make revision easier.

02 / The proposed experience

The idea was for a system to capture a lesson, transcribe and summarise it, then create revision material shaped around a student’s preferences. I thought about cameras and AI as possible parts of that system, but the idea raises important questions about consent, privacy, access, and what should—or should not—be recorded in a classroom.

03 / What I tried

I was still learning how to code. My notes say that I explored APIs and used AI tools to help build parts of the idea. They do not document a complete, tested, or deployed classroom product, so this page describes ClassEcho as an AI Thon idea rather than a finished system.

The idea was not selected for an award. I still learned how to start with a real user problem, think about how AI might help, and begin exploring an implementation without already knowing everything.

04 / What it would take

Before any real classroom trial, the project would need clear permission from students, educators, and families; a privacy-first approach to recordings; secure handling or deletion of data; and a way to check that summaries are accurate and useful.

Those are part of the design, not extras. A tool for learning should support the classroom without quietly collecting more than it needs.

05 / What remains open

Could the idea work without recording a whole class? How could a student check a summary against the original lesson? What personalisation is genuinely helpful? Could a simple, consent-based prototype test the value before adding more AI?

Those questions are where I would start if I return to ClassEcho.
