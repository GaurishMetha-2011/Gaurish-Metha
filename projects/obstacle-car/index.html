THE DESIGN GOAL

How can the components for an obstacle-avoidance car fit together on a single board?

01 / From parts to a board

I used KiCad to make a PCB layout for an obstacle-avoidance car, with space for the components to fit on the board. The work focused on bringing the parts together in a board design rather than presenting each small electronics experiment as a separate project.

02 / Footprint and placement

I created a custom footprint as part of the KiCad work, then arranged component footprints on the board. My notes do not preserve the exact component model, pad dimensions, or footprint drawing, so this page does not recreate or guess those details.

03 / What the records support

I no longer remember the exact component list or circuit connections. Without the original schematic and PCB files, I can’t accurately describe the sensor, controller, motor-driving, or power architecture, or claim that a fabricated car was tested.

Design scope. The known outcome is a KiCad board layout intended to fit the components. The graphic above is an illustration, not a rendering of the actual PCB.
