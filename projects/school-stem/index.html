THE BEGINNING

School exhibitions gave me a reason to take a science idea, make a model, and stand beside it long enough to explain how I thought it worked.

01 / A bridge and a wheel

My early school STEM work included a hydraulic bridge and a Ferris wheel model. I remember bringing these kinds of builds to school exhibitions and being excited to demonstrate them.

The source notes do not preserve full specifications or construction details for these models, so this page keeps their description at the level I can support: early school exhibition projects that explored moving structures and mechanisms.

02 / From model to explanation

An exhibition project is not only the object. I also had to think about how to set it up, what someone looking at it might ask, and how to explain the idea in a way that made sense.

Those early presentations became part of the story: I liked the making, but I also liked getting to show the result and talk through it.
