CURRENT CURIOSITIES / FUTURE STUDY

Things I want
to understand.

These are interests and directions for future learning—not claims of expertise or professional experience.

01

Electronics & embedded systems

I’m drawn to the point where software meets the physical world: sensing, control, and building a device that does something useful.

02

PCB design / KiCad

I have started exploring PCB design and want to learn the full path from circuit idea to a board that can be assembled and tested.

03

Semiconductors & materials

AETHER led me to read about luminescent materials and carbon quantum dots. I want to understand the evidence and limitations behind these materials more carefully.

04

Robotics, ROS 2 & SLAM

I have begun exploring ROS 2 and Gazebo at a basic level. Robotics raises the kind of connected questions I like: sensors, perception, movement, and software.

05

RF engineering

I am curious about radio systems and how information moves through the air. Learning the technical foundations and operating within local rules are both part of that interest.

06

EV power electronics

Power conversion and the systems behind electric vehicles are areas I would like to explore as I develop a stronger electronics foundation.

07

Computer & network systems

Servers, storage, virtualisation, home automation, and network design interest me as connected systems to study and experiment with.

Interests can change.

The point is not to collect a permanent list. It is to keep learning enough to make better questions—and, eventually, better experiments.
