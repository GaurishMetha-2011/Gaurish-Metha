FROM MECHANICAL BUILDS TO ENGINEERING

How one interest
led to another.

Making started with mechanical construction sets. From there, I followed questions through electronics, computing, creative technology, and project ideas.

  1. COVID PERIOD / INDIA

    Building with MECHANIX sets

    My parents bought me several construction sets with metal strips, rods, screws, nuts, and other parts. I spent hours assembling them into mechanical structures and models. This was my first interest in making things.

    Read about the first builds ↗
  2. LATER / OMAN

    Electronics and physical STEM builds

    An electronics kit brought me to motors and circuits. I also made school exhibition models, including a hydraulic bridge and a Ferris wheel.

    Read about the electronics stage ↗
  3. EARLY EXPERIMENTS

    A sweeper idea, a PIR sensor, and Arduino

    I adapted a remote-controlled car with sweeping motors, then followed a sensor I mistook for a disco light into PIR sensors and Arduino experiments.

    Read about the experiments ↗
  4. AI EXPLORATION

    Generative AI and local models

    Trying generative AI led me to prompt writing, image and video tools, and exploring local models with Ollama and ComfyUI.

    Read about AI exploration ↗
  5. COMPUTING

    Hardware, operating systems, and software

    My first laptop sparked questions about computer components, operating systems, software, and what different hardware can run. Comparing PC parts did not mean I had built a custom PC.

    Read about computing ↗
  6. CONNECTED SYSTEMS

    Servers, networks, and smart homes

    I explored NAS, networking, virtualisation, server software, and smart-home systems. Much of this remained research, diagrams, or limited experiments rather than complete installations.

    Read about connected systems ↗
  7. CREATIVE TECHNOLOGY / SCHOOL

    Making experiences for other people

    For the school’s 50th-anniversary exhibition, I contributed VR worlds, animations, clips, presentations, and demonstrations. I also edited school teasers and helped with other displays.

    Read about school contributions ↗
  8. PROJECT IDEAS

    ClassEcho and AETHER

    ClassEcho began as an AI classroom-assistant idea. A separate solar-window concept developed from AURS into AETHER. Each has a case study describing its scope and what remains untested.

    Explore the project case studies ↗
  9. CURRENT DIRECTION

    Electronics, embedded systems, and engineering

    I’m building my foundations in electronics and want to bring hardware and software together while learning more about embedded systems and semiconductor technology. A current design exercise is a KiCad PCB for an obstacle-avoidance car.
